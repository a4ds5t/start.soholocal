# v1.5

Since v1.4: a Quick start window explains the search engine, the AI links and the three pages, and a first visit gets a short hint that points to the settings. The reset buttons look and behave more clearly, and the settings link to the changelog.

## Highlights

- **Quick start**: a window that explains the basics on one page. The search engine icon is shown with a red arrow: each click on it goes to the next engine (DuckDuckGo, Kagi, Brave or Google), and your choice is remembered. It also covers adding AI links and choosing Minimal, Standard or Advanced. It opens from the first-visit card, from the basic settings and from Help.
- **First visit**: a small card under the top-right icons points to the settings and to the Quick start. It shows only until you read the Quick start, open the settings or press the ? key. After that it does not come back.
- **Reset**: the two reset buttons sit under one red heading at the bottom of Advanced, at the same height as the other buttons. Manage data sits beside them, without a red frame.
- **Weather places**: the count under the places is gone. When the list is full, the add field says so.
- **Version and changelog**: the bottom of the basic settings shows the version and the date of the last change, with a link to the changelog.

## Fixes

- The Quick start has its headings in place, so the HTML check passes.
